FAQ

Questions owners usually ask.

How much does a website cost?

A professional CyberSpace Local website starts at $499 for the core package — a custom-designed, mobile-ready site with up to five pages, a contact form, and a solid SEO foundation. If you need custom functionality or a larger build, we’ll quote that separately.

How long does a website take?

Most core websites are completed within a few weeks, depending on how quickly we receive your content and feedback. We’ll give you a clear timeline after we learn a little about your business.

Do I need to provide photos?

If you have photos of your work, team, or location, we’ll use them. If you don’t, we can work with appropriate imagery that fits your business while you gather your own.

Can you help with my domain?

Yes. If you already have a domain, we’ll help you point it to your new site. If you don’t, we can walk you through getting one set up.

What does the $149/month plan include?

Growth & Management covers website hosting and upkeep, ongoing updates, minor content changes, search optimization improvements, new pages when they make sense, AI-search optimization, and priority support. It is not unlimited custom development — larger requests are quoted separately.

Can I make changes after launch?

Yes. Small updates are handled through the Growth & Management plan. If you’d rather not subscribe, one-off changes can be quoted as needed.

What types of businesses do you work with?

CyberSpace Local is focused on local and small businesses across a wide range of industries — trades, home services, professional practices, hospitality, and other companies that serve customers in a real place.
